# MECHANICS FOR NEW AGE (M4NA)

### Redefining the Foundations of Mechanics & Cosmology

Explore. Question. Discover. Rethink the Universe.

Mechanics for New Age (M4NA) is a scientific exploration platform dedicated to re-examining the foundations of classical mechanics and exploring new perspectives on motion, applied force, mass, energy, quantum mechanics, relativity and cosmology.

Discover original concepts, theoretical discussions, mathematical relationships and interactive scientific explorations.

Our Mission: To encourage scientific inquiry and explore the fundamental principles of the physical universe.
