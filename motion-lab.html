M4NA Mathematical Relationships

f = kv

v = f/k

sᵢ = vᵢtᵢ

Fᵢ = fᵢtᵢ

S = Σ(vᵢtᵢ)

F = Σ(fᵢtᵢ)

V = S/T

F = kS

Calculation note: The calculator treats each row as a separate operation. It calculates velocity as f/k, travelling distance as vt, and force quantity as ft. Total values are obtained by summing the operations. These calculations implement the proposed M4NA model; they do not by themselves establish its empirical validity.
