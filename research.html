M4NA SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH

Exploring the Foundations of Mechanics

A platform for theoretical exploration, mathematical investigation and scientific examination through the Mechanics for New Age framework.

01 — Research Vision

Mechanics for New Age (M4NA) provides a platform for developing and examining a proposed force-centred approach to understanding motion.

Its research direction focuses on the relationship between applied force, motion value, travelling distance and successive operations.

The objective is to establish a structured framework for examining fundamental concepts of mechanics through theoretical propositions, mathematical relationships and scientific discussion.

02 — Research Domains

DOMAIN 01

Classical Mechanics

Re-examining established mechanical concepts and investigating alternative approaches to understanding motion.

DOMAIN 02

Applied Force

Investigating the proposed operating role of applied force in producing, rebuilding and maintaining motion.

DOMAIN 03

Motion Analysis

Examining velocity, actual travelling distance and successive operations.

DOMAIN 04

Mathematical Formulation

Developing and examining the mathematical expressions proposed within M4NA.

DOMAIN 05

Quantum & Relativity

A future research direction examining questions relating to quantum phenomena and relativity.

DOMAIN 06

Cosmology & Universe

A future direction for exploring fundamental questions concerning cosmology and the universe.

03 — Theoretical Investigations

M4NA research is organised around theoretical propositions that require systematic examination, mathematical consistency and scientific scrutiny.

Research propositions are presented as developing concepts of the M4NA framework. Their scientific validity and explanatory scope remain subjects for investigation, analysis and independent evaluation.
