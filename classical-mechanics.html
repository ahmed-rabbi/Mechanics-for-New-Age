M4NA CLASSICAL MECHANICS

Re-examining the Foundations of Classical Mechanics

Exploring the proposed relationship between applied force, motion value, travelling distance and successive operations.

01 — Introduction

Classical Mechanics has traditionally provided the mathematical foundation for examining the motion of physical objects.

Mechanics for New Age (M4NA) proposes a force-centred framework for re-examining fundamental concepts of mechanics and the operational relationship between applied force and motion.

The central objective of M4NA Classical Mechanics is to examine motion through its operating cause, motion value and actual travelling distance.

02 — Fundamental Concepts

Applied Force

Applied force is the operating cause of an object's motion, producing, rebuilding or maintaining its motion value and motion quantity, and operating the object in a specific direction.

Motion Value

Motion value represents the velocity or rate of an object's motion.

Motion Quantity

Motion quantity represents the actual travelling distance of an object.

Operation

An operation provides a basis for examining the corresponding force, velocity, time and travelling distance.

03 — M4NA Definition of Motion

Motion is the outcome of an applied force which provides a real picture of the traverse of an object by representing its motion nature, value (velocity/rate) and quantity (travelling distance) within successive intervals of the same operation, different operations, or various segments of the same or different intervals.

This definition establishes the conceptual foundation for examining motion through the operating conditions of an object and its successive movements.

04 — Force Value and Motion Value

M4NA proposes a proportional relationship between force value and motion value.

f = kv

f = Force Value
v = Motion Value (Velocity)
k = Universal Proportionality Constant

The corresponding time-based expression is:

f/t = kv/t

Within the proposed framework, this expression is examined in relation to an instant or a specified duration of operation.

05 — Successive Operations

M4NA examines motion through successive operations, allowing the corresponding force value, velocity and travelling distance to be represented individually.

sᵢ = vᵢtᵢ
Fᵢ = fᵢtᵢ
Fᵢ = ksᵢ

Here, each operation is represented by its corresponding velocity, force value, time and travelling distance.

06 — Total Force and Total Travelling Distance

For multiple successive operations, M4NA proposes the following relationships:

F = Σ(fᵢtᵢ)
S = Σsᵢ = Σ(vᵢtᵢ)
F = kS

The total operating time is represented by:

T = Σtᵢ

The average velocity across the total travelling distance and operating time is:

V = Σ(vᵢtᵢ) / Σtᵢ

The proposed relationship connects total force quantity, total travelling distance and average velocity across successive operations.
