LAB 06 — Examining Instantaneous Force and Velocity

1. Fundamental M4NA Relationship

This laboratory examines the proposed M4NA relationship between the instantaneous applied-force value and the corresponding instantaneous velocity.

f = kv

f = applied-force value   |   v = instantaneous velocity   |   k = proportionality constant

M4NA approach: Each instantaneous force value corresponds to a velocity value under the selected proportionality constant. The calculator below demonstrates this mathematical relationship as proposed within the M4NA framework.

5. M4NA Mathematical Expression

f = kv
f/t = kv/t

The first expression represents the proposed instantaneous relationship. The time-form expression may be considered in relation to an instant or a duration, according to the operation being examined.
