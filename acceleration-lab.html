01 — Purpose of the Laboratory

This laboratory examines the nature of acceleration by observing how the velocity values and travelling distances of an object change through successive operations.

M4NA approach: Motion is examined through the corresponding applied-force value, velocity value and actual travelling distance in successive operations. Changes in velocity values reveal the motion pattern across those operations.

02 — Fundamental Relationship

The M4NA framework proposes a relationship between the applied-force value and the corresponding velocity value.

f = kv

Where:

  • f = applied-force value
  • k = proportionality constant
  • v = corresponding velocity value

By comparing successive velocity values, the laboratory identifies increasing, decreasing, unchanged and mixed motion patterns.

04 — Understanding the Motion Patterns

Increasing Pattern

Successive velocity values increase from one operation to the next.

2 → 4 → 6 → 8

Decreasing Pattern

Successive velocity values decrease from one operation to the next.

8 → 6 → 4 → 2

Unchanged Pattern

The velocity value remains unchanged through successive operations.

4 → 4 → 4 → 4

Mixed Pattern

The velocity values increase and decrease at different stages of successive operations.

2 → 4 → 6 → 4 → 2

05 — M4NA Mathematical Expressions

f = kv
sᵢ = vᵢtᵢ
F = Σfᵢtᵢ
S = Σvᵢtᵢ
V = S/T
F = kS

These expressions are presented as relationships within the proposed M4NA framework.

06 — Research Perspective

The purpose of this laboratory is to examine motion patterns rather than relying only on a single initial and final velocity value. Each operation contributes its own force value, velocity value, duration and travelling distance.

Comparing successive operations provides a structured way to examine how the proposed applied-force relationship corresponds to changes in motion.
