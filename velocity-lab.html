01 — Purpose of the Laboratory

This laboratory examines velocity as the value of motion and travelling distance as the quantity of motion. It explores how both are represented through successive operations of an object.

M4NA perspective: Velocity represents the value or rate of motion, while actual travelling distance represents the quantity of motion. Each operation has a corresponding velocity value and travelling distance over its duration.

02 — Fundamental Velocity Relationship

The M4NA framework proposes a direct relationship between the applied-force value and the corresponding velocity value.

f = kv

Where:

  • f = applied-force value
  • k = proportionality constant
  • v = velocity value
s = vt

Travelling distance for an operation is represented by its velocity value multiplied by its duration.

05 — Velocity, Distance and Time

Each operation contributes its own travelling distance. The total travelling distance is obtained by adding the distances from all operations.

sᵢ = vᵢtᵢ
S = Σvᵢtᵢ
T = Σtᵢ
V = S/T

The average velocity value represents the total travelling distance divided by the total duration of the recorded operations.

06 — M4NA Mathematical Expressions

f = kv
sᵢ = vᵢtᵢ
F = Σfᵢtᵢ
S = Σvᵢtᵢ
V = S/T
F = kS
F/T = kV

07 — Research Perspective

The laboratory focuses on the operational relationship between velocity values, travelling distances and time. It records the individual values for each operation rather than treating a complete journey as one undifferentiated event.

Key examination: Different successive velocity patterns can produce different motion histories. Therefore, examining only the average velocity value does not reveal every detail of the successive motion pattern.
