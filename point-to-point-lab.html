01 — Purpose of the Laboratory

This laboratory examines how an object's motion can be represented through successive operations, each having its own velocity value, duration and actual travelling distance.

M4NA perspective: A representation based only on an initial point and a final point does not, by itself, describe every stage of the object's actual travel. Successive operations provide additional information about the motion pattern and the distance travelled during each operation.

02 — Point-to-Point Representation

A point-to-point representation identifies two positions. However, the two endpoints alone do not reveal how much travelling distance was covered along the route or how motion changed during the journey.

Initial Point → Final Point

The M4NA laboratory instead records the successive operations and adds their actual travelling distances.

06 — Research Perspective

The central examination of this laboratory is whether a description based only on two endpoints adequately represents the nature of motion throughout a journey.

The successive-operation approach records each operation separately and calculates its travelling distance. The total distance is then obtained by adding the distances travelled in all recorded operations.

Key examination: Two journeys may share the same starting and ending positions while having different actual travelling distances and different successive motion patterns.
