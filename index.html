What is M4NA?

Mechanics for New Age is a developing scientific framework and publishing platform. It presents original concepts, mathematical relationships and discussions concerning motion, applied force and the foundations of mechanics.

Our Mission

To encourage scientific inquiry, challenge conventional thinking and contribute to the continuing exploration of nature's fundamental principles.
