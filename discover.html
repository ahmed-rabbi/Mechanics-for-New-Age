01 — What is M4NA?

Mechanics for New Age (M4NA) is a proposed scientific framework that seeks to re-examine the foundations of classical mechanics through a force-centred approach to understanding motion.

The framework focuses on the relationship between applied force, motion value, travelling distance and the successive operations through which the motion of an object is examined.

M4NA places applied force at the centre of its proposed explanation of an object's motion, examining how force operates to produce, rebuild or maintain motion.

02 — The Fundamental Perspective

M4NA approaches motion through two connected dimensions: motion value and motion quantity.

Motion Value

Motion value represents the velocity or rate of an object's motion.

Motion Quantity

Motion quantity represents the actual travelling distance of an object.

Operating Force

Applied force is examined as the operating cause of motion within the M4NA framework.

03 — The M4NA Concept of Motion

Motion is the outcome of an applied force which provides a real picture of the traverse of an object by representing its motion nature, value (velocity/rate) and quantity (travelling distance) within successive intervals of the same operation, different operations, or various segments of the same or different intervals.

This definition establishes the conceptual basis for examining motion through its operating conditions, velocity and actual travelling distance.

04 — Force and Motion Relationship

The M4NA framework proposes a proportional relationship between force value and motion value.

f = kv

f = Force Value   |   v = Motion Value (Velocity)   |   k = Proportionality Constant

The framework also examines the corresponding relationship across time and successive operations.
